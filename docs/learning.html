Activity 4

Map source evidence into a canonical fixture

Choose one example above and trace it from its catalog page into the prepared source-neutral record. The fixture must retain source identity while giving every feature one stable shape.

Review canonical contracts

Record from the source

  • Provider, external identifier, exact URL, and retrieval date.
  • Auction house, auction, lot number, category, and status.
  • Original estimate, realized price when present, and currency.
  • Descriptions, images, and explicitly missing fields.

Verify in the prepared fixture

  • The canonical identifier stays stable across repeated imports.
  • Original money and source lineage are preserved.
  • Search and valuation do not read provider-specific payloads.
  • The fixture contains no contact information or credentials.
