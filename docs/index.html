Customer journeys

These videos show the intended buyer and seller interactions. They support the written assignments; they do not replace the weekly task descriptions.

Buyer outcome

Describe, discover, and investigate

A buyer starts without an account and writes in Hebrew or English. The Assistant asks simple questions, remembers the answers, and finds current and past auction items. It explains each match. The buyer can open one item and inspect its source, auction house, and price history.

Seller outcome

Document, compare, value, and submit

A seller starts without an account and adds photos. The Assistant asks for facts such as size, condition, marks, and history. The seller can fix the facts. When there is enough sale data, the Assistant shows a supported price range and similar past sales. The seller can choose an auction house and send an enquiry to Monabid.

The buyer journey is the primary traction path. The seller journey must still deliver comparable evidence and a supported indication or refusal before requesting contact.

Scope boundary

The standalone assistant discovers, explains, captures qualified intent, creates internal enquiries, and keeps source references for future Dibzel integration.

Required cycle

  • Anonymous bilingual conversations with RTL/LTR support.
  • Real buyer search, explained results, item evidence, and honest unmatched-demand capture.
  • Seller-confirmed facts, private photos, comparable evidence, supported indication or refusal, catalog draft, and house options.
  • Optional post-value buyer and seller contact with separate service consent.
  • Prepared source-neutral fixtures, deterministic logic, validated AI, and guided fallback.
  • A small staff operations dashboard for manual follow-up.

Explicit exclusions

  • Saved searches, automated alerts, notifications, email delivery, and scheduled jobs.
  • Live Bright Data refresh, R2, production staff authorization, automated retention, and Dibzel writeback.
  • Bidding, payments, bidder approval, KYC, and live-auction mutation.
  • Completed consignments, automatic auction-house contact, or a full CRM.
  • Image interpretation, investment returns, invented history, certified appraisals, and guaranteed prices.

Future work Excluded capabilities can be reconsidered after all 14 owned features work, are tested, and are documented.

Private anonymous profiles

Useful information is collected progressively, after the product has demonstrated value.

1 · Start anonymously

A random browser/session identifier owns the profile. Language is captured first. Preferred Hebrew grammatical form can be masculine, feminine, neutral, or unspecified; it is never inferred.

2 · Deliver value first

Browsing, search results, item evidence, seller comparables, and a supported indication or refusal do not require contact details. Nonessential questions can be skipped.

3 · Choose help and retain control

A buyer or seller who requests manual help provides a preferred name, email or phone, and explicit service-contact consent. Marketing consent stays separate. Visitors can review, update, and delete their current profile and unsubmitted evidence.

Minimum contact

  • Browsing and receiving value: none.
  • Buyer help request or seller enquiry: preferred name, email or phone, and service-contact consent.

Isolation

  • Demonstrations use synthetic contact information and evidence.
  • Profiles and uploads never cross visitors.
  • Contact data and full conversations stay out of ordinary logs.

Durable product policies

Deterministic logic owns consequential decisions; AI may understand and explain.

Search

PostgreSQL text search, strict filters, curated Hebrew/English aliases, and explainable deterministic ranking. Unsupported data is presented honestly.

Valuation

Require at least three credible realized-price comparables. Preserve original currencies and normalize to USD with a dated fixture. Record comparables, weights, outliers, exchange rates, and confidence. Never invent precision.

Real AI with safe fallback

Use the prepared OpenAI-compatible endpoint, validate structured output, and fall back to guided input. Contact details and images are not sent. Search, comparables, currency, valuation, and confidence stay deterministic.

Private profiles and photos

Profiles and local photos belong to one anonymous session. Visitors can review and delete their information. Synthetic contact details and evidence are used publicly.

Recommendations

Recommend one active prepared auction house and relevant alternatives using available evidence. Make no unsupported claims about commission, shipping, acceptance, or guaranteed value.

Fixture-first operation

CI calls no Bright Data, BidSpirit, Dibzel, hosted AI, storage, or email service. Students receive prepared snapshots and provider configuration; they do not investigate external systems.

Conversation to evidence to optional help

Anonymous bilingual conversation, useful clarification, preserved criteria, explained current/historical results, refinement, result cards, item evidence, and only then an optional manual-help request. A second scenario records useful unmatched demand without inventing inventory.

Seller

Evidence to enquiry

Bilingual intake, multiple local uploads, seller corrections, comparable evidence, supported range or honest expert-review refusal, house options, catalog draft, and only then contact/consent and internal enquiry.

Internal
